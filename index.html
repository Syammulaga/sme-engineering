ABOUT SME

Engineering knowledge. Practical design.

SME Engineering & Design is an independent engineering and drafting service focused on delivering clear, buildable and professional design documentation.

Our capabilities combine architectural, civil, structural, piping, MEP, industrial and Oil & Gas design with CAD, 3D modelling, RLMU and As-Built conversion.
